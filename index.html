Built around a real analyst question

As a sales operations manager, I need a trusted view of sales, a way to diagnose the drivers and a transparent list of follow-up questions—so I can focus investigation instead of guessing.

The bundled data is synthetic. Figures are for demonstrating the workflow, not business outcomes.

Metric note: “Gross sales on retained lines” includes positive retained postage/shipping charges and excludes cancelled invoices, non-positive quantity/price rows and invalid dates. It is not audited net revenue. Currency is not assumed.
